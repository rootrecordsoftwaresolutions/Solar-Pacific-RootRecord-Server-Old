# American history (desk pack)

Spine of the United States as a political story. Indigenous nations are many;
do not flatten them into one tribe or one date. Hawaiʻi 1893–1959: use
[hawaiian.md](hawaiian.md) first.

## Before the republic

- **Indigenous nations** — peoples already here. Treaties, removals, and wars
  are specific. If the pack has no nation named, say so.
- **1492** — Columbus reaches the Caribbean. Not “discovery of America” as an
  empty continent.
- **1565** — St. Augustine (Spanish Florida). **1607** Jamestown. **1620**
  Plymouth. Other colonies follow (Dutch, French, English, Spanish, Russian
  in the north Pacific later).
- **1754–1763** — French and Indian War / Seven Years’ War in North America.
  Britain tightens control after.

## Founding

- **1775–1783** — American Revolutionary War. **4 July 1776** Declaration of
  Independence. **1783** Treaty of Paris.
- **1787** — Constitution drafted. **1788** ratified. **1789** government
  under Washington. **1791** Bill of Rights.
- **1803** — Louisiana Purchase. **1804–1806** Lewis and Clark.
- **1812–1815** — War of 1812.

## Expansion, slavery, Civil War

- **1830** — Indian Removal Act. Trail of Tears follows in the 1830s
  (Cherokee and others). Do not invent death counts here.
- **1846–1848** — Mexican-American War; Mexican Cession.
- **1848** — Seneca Falls; gold in California.
- **1861–1865** — Civil War. **1863** Emancipation Proclamation (wartime,
  rebel states). **1865** 13th Amendment abolishes slavery. Lincoln
  assassinated April 1865.
- **14th (1868)** and **15th (1870)** Amendments — citizenship and (male)
  voting. Reconstruction ends **1877** in the usual textbook beat
  (Compromise of 1877). Jim Crow follows.

## Overseas and the 20th century

- **1890** — Wounded Knee. Census “frontier closed” is a later slogan, not a
  fact that Native nations vanished.
- **1898** — Spanish-American War. Philippines, Guam, Puerto Rico. **Hawaiʻi
  annexed the same year** — Hawaiian pack.
- **1917–1918** — US in World War I. **19th Amendment (1920)** — women’s
  suffrage.
- **1929** — crash. **1930s** New Deal.
- **7 Dec 1941** — Pearl Harbor. US in World War II through **1945**.
- **1945 on** — United Nations; Cold War with the Soviet Union.
- **1954** — Brown v. Board. **1964** Civil Rights Act. **1965** Voting
  Rights Act. **1965–1973** major US combat in Vietnam (war longer on both
  ends).
- **1969** — Moon landing.

## Later spine

- **1991** — Soviet Union dissolves; Gulf War.
- **11 September 2001** — attacks; Afghanistan and later Iraq wars.
- Constitutions, amendments, and court cases after that: only if named in
  the ask. This pack does not track live elections.

## Not on this pack

Live vote totals. Invented casualty figures. Full Native nation histories
not listed above. Operator machine history (`ecosystem-history`).
