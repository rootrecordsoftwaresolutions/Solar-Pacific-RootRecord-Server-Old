# Hawaiian history (desk pack)

Orthography: kahakō and ʻokina on the names below. ASCII aliases are matching
only. Settlement years are scholarly ranges, not a single dig date.

Place names and districts: `geography`. Language: `hawaiian-glossary`. Live

lot facts: `fern-forest`.

## Peopling

- **Polynesian voyaging** — Hawaiʻi is the northern point of the Polynesian
  triangle. People reached these islands by canoe from the South Pacific.
- **Settlement range** — often given around the **11th–13th centuries CE**.
  Older writing used earlier centuries. Do not pick a single year.
- **Society** — island chiefdoms (moku, ahupuaʻa as land divisions in later
  recorded form). Do not invent kapu tables or god lists that are not asked
  for and not on this desk.

## Contact and kingdom

- **1778** — James Cook’s ships reach **Kauaʻi** (Waimea). Later returns; Cook
  is killed at Kealakekua, Hawaiʻi island, **1779**.
- **Kamehameha I** — wars of unification through the 1790s; **Battle of
  Nuʻuanu (1795)** on Oʻahu. **Kauaʻi and Niʻihau** come under the kingdom
  **c. 1810**. This is the Hawaiian Kingdom.
- **1819** — after Kamehameha I’s death, the **ʻaikapu** eating kapu is ended
  (Liholiho / Kamehameha II, Kaʻahumanu, Keōpūolani in the recorded account).
- **1820** — first company of American Protestant missionaries arrives.
- **1826 / 1840s** — written Hawaiian spreads; early constitutions. **1840**
  constitution under Kamehameha III (Kauikeaouli).
- **Great Māhele (1848)** — land division into crown, government, chief, and
  commoner claims. Do not invent acre counts.
- **Reciprocity Treaty (1875)** — sugar access to the US market; later Pearl
  Harbor lease (1887) sits in the same sugar-and-navy thread.

## Overthrow to territory

- **Kalākaua** — elected king **1874**. **Bayonet Constitution (1887)** strips
  much royal authority under armed pressure.
- **Liliʻuokalani** — queen **1891**. Attempts to restore a constitution.
- **17 January 1893** — the queen is overthrown by a local committee with
  support from US marines landed from USS Boston. Provisional government.
  President Cleveland later calls the action improper; Congress does not
  restore the queen.
- **1894** — Republic of Hawaiʻi (Sanford Dole president).
- **1898** — Newlands Resolution: US annexation during the Spanish-American
  War. **1900** — Organic Act; Territory of Hawaiʻi.
- **Apology** — US Public Law **103-150 (1993)** acknowledges the 1893
  overthrow. It is a resolution, not a restoration of the kingdom.

## War and statehood

- **7 December 1941** — Imperial Japan attacks **Pearl Harbor**, Oʻahu. US
  enters World War II. Martial law in the Territory.
- **21 August 1959** — Hawaiʻi admitted as the **50th state**.
- **Hawaiian Renaissance (1970s on)** — language, voyaging (*Hōkūleʻa* 1976),
  and political organizing return to public life. **Office of Hawaiian
  Affairs** is a state agency from this era. Do not invent membership counts.
