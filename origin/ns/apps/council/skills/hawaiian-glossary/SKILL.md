# ʻŌlelo Hawaiʻi (desk pack)

Orthography: kahakō and ʻokina on the canonical spelling. ASCII aliases are matching only.

Use dictionary hits in the prompt when present. If a token is not in this pack or the local dictionary, say you don’t know. Do not invent etymology, gods, chants, or secret codes. Do not lecture unless asked.

## Place / this ʻāina

- **Hawaiʻi** (hawaii) — the islands; also Hawaiʻi island (the Big Island). Fern Forest is on Hawaiʻi island; parcel desk `fern-forest`.
- **Kīlauea** (kilauea) — active volcano, Hawaiʻi island; Root Record “Kīlauea Alerts”. **Not “Kiweh.”**
- **Hilo**, **Puna**, **Kaʻū** (kau), **Kona** — districts; do not invent boundaries.
- **Mauna Loa**; **Mauna Kea** (Mauna a Wākea is the respectful name — note it, don’t lecture).
- **Oʻahu** (oahu), **Maui**, **Kauaʻi** (kauai), **Molokaʻi** (molokai), **Lānaʻi** (lanai), **Niʻihau** (niihau), **Kahoʻolawe** (kahoolawe).

## Everyday

- **aloha** — greeting / love / compassion. Don’t over-explain.
- **mahalo** — thanks.
- **ʻāina** (aina) — land, that which feeds. Bare “aina” is a different dictionary word.
- **kai** — sea. **mauna** — mountain. **wahine** / **kāne** (kane) — woman / man.
- **ʻohana** (ohana) — family. Don’t Disney-expand.
- **kamaʻāina** (kamaaina) — local to this place. **malihini** — visitor.
- **ʻōlelo Hawaiʻi** (olelo hawaii) — Hawaiian language.
- **pau** — finished. **wikiwiki** — quick. **kōkua** (kokua) — help.
- **mauka** / **makai** — toward mountain / toward sea.

## Not Hawaiian

- **Kiweh, Kileau, Kiwea** are not Hawaiian. Treat as likely **Kīlauea** misspelling; say so.
- Do not invent gods, chants, or “ancient meanings” for unknown strings.
- Do not claim live eruption, watts, or SOC unless a live check exists.
