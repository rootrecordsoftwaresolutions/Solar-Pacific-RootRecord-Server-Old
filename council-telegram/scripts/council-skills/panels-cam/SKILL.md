# panels-cam

Night Owl **Rear Shed** still (solar panels). River car 12V for camera power.

Owner or open-exec: “show me the panels / camera(s)”, “solar panels”, “rear shed cam”, “night owl”.

`--show` (default from council) powers car if dark, grabs RTSP ch1, prints `PANELS_PHOTO=…`, then turns car off unless drives hold it. Never AC.
