What is Root Record

What is Root Record?

Root Record (RootRecord) builds multi-device software for real operations: weather, Kīlauea awareness, business tools, and the account that ties them together. The public site is rootrecord.info. One membership can cover RootRecord apps and RootMC.

What products do you ship?

Kīlauea Alerts, Weather Manager, and Business Manager are live on Google Play and as web apps at *.rootrecord.info. See Services and Pricing for the full list.

RootMC

What is RootMC?

RootMC is the Minecraft survival network. Join at play.rootmc.net (Java). Server info, player tools, and the market live on rootmc.net. Play page: play.rootmc.net.

Do I need a RootRecord account to play?

You can connect to the server with Minecraft. Linking a RootRecord account unlocks Discord verification and member perks. Sign in at /account and link Discord from there.

Accounts

How do I create an account?

Use /account-signup to register, then sign in at /account. The same credentials work in RootRecord apps where sign-in is supported.

I forgot my password.

Reset it at /account-reset. Request a reset email, then open the link or enter the code and a new password. Sign-in stays on /account only.

How do I verify Discord?

Sign in, then use Discord verification or the Discord section on your account page. Join the RootRecord Discord so @Verified can be assigned.

Billing

What plans exist?

Free, Monthly, and Lifetime. Plain-language comparison is on Pricing. Manage or change a plan from Billing after you sign in.

How do renewals and cancellations work?

Recurring plans bill through Stripe until you cancel. Use Billing (or the Stripe customer portal linked there) to update payment or cancel. Legal detail is in Terms.

Kīlauea Alerts

What is Kīlauea Alerts?

A Hawaiʻi Island app for Kīlauea dashboards, earthquakes, weather, USGS volcano notices, and NWS alerts. Product page: Kīlauea Alerts. Web: kilauea.rootrecord.info. Android: Google Play.

Is it official USGS or HVO?

No. Feeds come from public USGS, HVO, and NWS sources. The app is a dashboard, not an official agency product. For life-safety decisions, follow official notices.

Ava status board

Where is the Ava status board?

Live host status, solar, and related boards are on ava.rootmc.net. Solar: ava.rootmc.net/solar. If those pages do not load, the Root Server is offline.

Files

Where are member Files?

Member file login is at ava.rootmc.net/files. Sign in with your RootRecord account. Public downloads (when posted) are separate from this member login.
