Add a custom domain

Optional. We add the domain to the Root Record Cloudflare account, attach it to hosting, and give you the nameservers to set at your registrar.

  1. Keep using your default Root Record URL — it works immediately after publish.
  2. Enter the domain you own (example: alexrs94.site).
  3. We create/link the zone in our Cloudflare account and attach it to Website Hosting.
  4. At your registrar, replace nameservers with the two Root Record Cloudflare NS values shown on this page (or the ones returned after add).
  5. DNS usually activates in minutes to a few hours. Status here flips to active when the ecosystem sees it live.
