# Events & referrals (templates)

Customize rewards to match your economy — be clear if items are cosmetic-only.

---

## Generic event announcement

```
**Event: {{EVENT_NAME}}**

**When:** {{DATE_TIME_TIMEZONE}}
**Where:** {{SERVER_IP}} · meet at {{LOCATION}}
**What:** {{EVENT_DESCRIPTION}}

**Rewards:** {{REWARD_LIST}}
**Rules:** {{WIKI_OR_RULES_URL}}

RSVP in thread · Questions in Discord
```
