# Hawaiʻi — zone overlay

Hawaiʻi Island first. Not one climate. USDA PHZM is winter min, not rain.

Look up the Rainfall Atlas pixel and the 2023 ZIP. Do not guess Hilo vs
Kawaihae vs Waimea vs the summits.

Truth and limits: `../../references/big-island.md`
`python3 ~/.ollama/skills/gardening/scripts/garden.py big-island`

## Official

- Rainfall Atlas of Hawaiʻi: http://rainfall.geography.hawaii.edu/
- USDA 2023 PHZM: https://planthardiness.ars.usda.gov/
- PRISM Hawaiʻi 400 m GIS: https://prism.oregonstate.edu/phzm/
- CTAHR, HDOA, NRCS PIA eFOTG, ARS Hilo TPGR, Hoʻolehua PMC, Statewide GIS soils
- Juvik, Singleton, Clarke 1978 exists (NOAA MLO). We have not got the PDF; do not recite a Köppen leftover from magazines.

## Polyculture default

Match local rain and elevation. CTAHR for the crop. HDOA for weeds.
Do not guess elevation climate.
