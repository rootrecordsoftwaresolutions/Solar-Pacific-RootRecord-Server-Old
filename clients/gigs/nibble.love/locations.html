Big Island Food Locations

Community drop points where you can give or get fresh food. Many are accessible 24 hours. Always check locally or call if you’re unsure about current availability.

Tip: Locations are often at churches and community centers. Be respectful of the space, leave areas tidy, and only take what you need so others can share too.
