Community Food Exchange

Share local meals. Reduce waste. Support neighbors.

Whether you’re dropping off extra mangoes from your tree or picking up eggs for breakfast, Nibble keeps the grid vibrating at a high frequency. No one needs to go hungry when good food is available.

🥬

Give Food

Have extra produce, baked goods, or prepared meals? Drop them at any Nibble location so they can feed someone nearby.

🧺

Get Food

Need groceries or a meal? Visit one of our community drop points — many are open 24 hours. Take what you need, leave what you can.

🤝

Build Community

Nibble is run by neighbors for neighbors. Managed by Marvin Franklin with support from Battery Bill Hawaii.
