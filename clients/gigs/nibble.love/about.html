About Nibble

A simple idea with deep roots in community care: make sure good food finds the people who need it.

Our Mission

Nibble is the local hub for the Community Food Exchange on the Big Island of Hawaiʻi. The mission is simple: give food or get food to help reduce waste and support our neighbors.

Too much good food goes uneaten while people nearby go without. Nibble connects the two — backyard harvests, extra groceries, prepared meals — with households that can use them. No bureaucracy. Just neighbors looking out for each other.

The platform (and the real-world drop points) are managed by our dedicated moderator, Marvin Franklin. The goal is to keep the community connected and the grid vibrating at a high frequency.

Why It Matters

On the Big Island, many of us grow more than we can eat — mangoes, avocados, bananas, eggs, herbs. At the same time, rising costs make it harder for families to keep the fridge full. Nibble is a practical, low-barrier way to close that gap.

It’s not a formal food bank or government program. It’s a grassroots exchange built on trust, respect, and the simple desire that nothing useful should be thrown away when someone nearby could use it.

Whether you’re dropping off a box of citrus or picking up a few meals for the week, you’re part of something that strengthens the whole community.
