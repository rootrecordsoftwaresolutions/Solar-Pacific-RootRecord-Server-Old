How Nibble Works

Simple community sharing. No membership, no paperwork, no cost — just neighbors helping neighbors reduce food waste.

Give Food

Have extra produce, eggs, baked goods, or prepared meals that would otherwise go to waste?

Prepare it safely

Package food in clean, sealed containers or bags. Label with what’s inside and the date if possible. Homegrown produce is especially welcome.

Drop it off

Take it to any listed Nibble location. Look for designated areas or coolers when available. Be respectful of church or community center property.

That’s it

You’ve just helped reduce waste and feed someone in your community. Mahalo!

Get Food

Need groceries, produce, or a meal? Nibble is here for you.

Find a location

Check the Locations page for current drop points in Mountain View, Keaau, Pahoa and nearby areas.

Visit anytime

Many sites are accessible 24 hours. Take only what you and your household need so others can share too.

Leave it better

If you can, leave something in return (even a kind note or extra produce from your garden). Keep the area clean.

Community Guidelines

🧼

Keep it clean & safe

Only share food you would eat yourself. Use clean packaging. Avoid highly perishable items without proper cooling if possible.

🙏

Be respectful

These locations are often churches and community centers. Treat the space and the people who maintain it with aloha.

⚖️

Share fairly

Take what you need. Leave what you can. The goal is that good food reaches as many neighbors as possible.

Questions about what to bring or current stock? Call or text Marvin at 317-721-5017. He’s happy to help coordinate.

Ready to share?

Browse the locations or get in touch if you’d like to start a new drop point in your neighborhood.
