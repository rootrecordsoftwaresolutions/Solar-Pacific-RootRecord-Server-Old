# Official databases

Use these. Do not substitute a blog for a zone, a soil map, or a plant identity.
Hawaiʻi first when the site is in the islands.

## Hardiness (what “zone” means)

| Store | What it is | URL |
| --- | --- | --- |
| USDA PHZM 2023 | Average annual extreme **minimum** winter temperature. 10 °F zones, 5 °F half-zones (1a–13b). | https://planthardiness.ars.usda.gov/ |
| PHZM map files | State/territory PNG downloads. | https://planthardiness.ars.usda.gov/pages/map-downloads |
| PRISM / OSU GIS | Grids, shapefiles, ZIP listings. Hawaiʻi and Puerto Rico at 400 m. | https://prism.oregonstate.edu/phzm/ |

Zones **12 and 13** were added so tropical / frost-free winters can be named. USDA says they appear on the **Hawaiʻi and Puerto Rico** maps. That is winter cold only. It is not a rainfall map. Hawaiʻi Island rain still varies sharply (Rainfall Atlas). See [big-island.md](big-island.md) for what is sourced and what is not.
