# Hawaiʻi overlay

Hawaiʻi Island first. Read [big-island.md](big-island.md) before a USDA zone.
Do not guess Hilo vs Waimea vs Volcano vs Kona vs Kawaihae.

## Zone vs rain

USDA 2023 PHZM is average annual **extreme minimum**. Zones **12** and **13**
show on the Hawaiʻi and Puerto Rico maps. **Up-slope is colder.** Summits are
not a tropical patio.

Rain, trades, and lava age are not in the PHZM. Use the Rainfall Atlas and
NRCS soils. Windward vs leeward changes the guild more than the half-zone
letter.

Do not recite “all but N climates.” Do not pin Köppen codes on towns from
memory.

## Systems to prefer (sourced) — then site them

- **Loʻi kalo** — needs water; CTAHR kalo. Not a dry leeward default.
- **Dryland field polyculture** — kalo / ʻuala / maiʻa as UH/CTAHR describe.
  Do not invent ahupuaʻa yields.
- **Ulu agroforest** — only where rain and drainage fit breadfruit; ARS Hilo
  holds *Artocarpus*.
- **Mixed agroforest** — NRCS PIA CPS 379 / 612.
- **Cover cocktails** — CTAHR SOAP; Wang elevation chart.
- **Shade coffee** — CTAHR coffee pubs. Default is shade, not a sun block.
  Site it where CTAHR puts the crop, not by a Köppen letter we did not map.
