# Prayer shapes

Write new words for the person in front of you. These are starting shapes, not scripts to dump unchanged. Keep them short. Do not promise outcomes.

## With someone (we)

Father, we are here. You see what we cannot fix. Hold us in this hour. Give us a quiet mind and a steady next step. Amen.

## For the person to say (I)

Lord, I am tired of carrying this alone. I set it down in front of you. Teach me to trust you today. Amen.

## Morning

God of this new day, thank you for breath and light. Walk with us in the work ahead. Keep us kind. Amen.

## Night

Lord, the day is done. Forgive what we got wrong. Guard our rest. Tomorrow is yours. Amen.

## Grief

God, this loss is heavy. We do not ask you to hurry the tears. Sit with us. Remember the one we love. Amen.

## Fear / storm / hard news

God our refuge, we are afraid. Be near. Keep our feet. Help us do the next right thing. Amen.

## Thanks

Father, thank you for what held today — the meal, the people, the mercy we did not earn. Amen.

## For a neighbor / family

Lord, we name them to you. Give them what they need. Help us love them without needing to control them. Amen.

## Before study

Open this Word to us. Let us hear it, not twist it. Amen.

## Lord's Prayer

When they ask for the Lord's Prayer, use Matthew 6:9–13 from [passages.md](passages.md). Do not modernize it into a copyrighted version unless they quote one first and only want help praying *with* those words they already have.
